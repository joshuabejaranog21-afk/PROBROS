Professional Water Damage Restoration Process

Every water damage emergency is unique. Our IICRC-certified team follows a proven, thorough process to stop additional loss and restore your property.

01

Emergency Water Extraction

We arrive with truck-mounted extraction equipment to remove standing water immediately, preventing structural damage and mold growth.

02

Structural Drying

Professional-grade dehumidifiers and air movers dry walls, subfloors, and framing. We monitor moisture levels throughout to ensure complete drying.

03

Cleaning & Sanitization

All affected surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial solutions to prevent mold and bacteria growth.

04

Restoration & Rebuild

From drywall replacement to flooring and paint, we restore your property to its pre-damage condition or better.

Water Damage FAQs

First, ensure everyone's safety and shut off the water source if it's safe. Call us immediately — we operate 24/7. While waiting for our arrival, move valuables away from water and document the damage with photos for your insurance.

Mold can develop within 24-48 hours of water exposure. That's why rapid response and thorough drying are critical. Our process includes sanitization and dehumidification to prevent mold growth.

Coverage depends on your policy and the source of the water. We work with your insurance company to maximize your claim. We bill them directly, so you're not stuck paying out of pocket.

Timeline depends on the extent of damage. Extraction and drying typically take 3-5 days. Reconstruction can take 2-4 weeks. We keep you updated at every stage.
