Pro Bros Restoration was founded in 2016 by Erick and Isai Bejarano. We're a small family business in a category that's usually neither — and we like it that way.

[ erick & isai · founder portrait ]
Why we do this

A different kind of restoration company.

Most people meet a restoration crew on the worst day of their year. By the time we're walking through your front door, you've already had a hard night. The least we can do is show up on time, tell you the truth, and not make you call a phone tree to do it.

We started Pro Bros because we'd watched too many of our own neighbors get treated like a claim number — handed off between subcontractors, billed for work that never happened, left to chase paperwork for months. We thought: there has to be a better way to do this. So we built it.

Almost ten years later, we still answer our own phone. Our crews still wear the same shirts. We still walk every job from first call to final paint. We're not the biggest restoration company in DFW. We don't want to be.

— Erick & Isai
Co-founders · Pro Bros Restoration
The road here

Nine years, one promise.

2016

Two brothers, one truck.

Erick and Isai start the company out of a single garage in Mesquite. First job: a slab leak under a neighbor's tile floor. They learn the trade by working harder than anyone else on the job site.

2018

IICRC certified.

Both brothers complete IICRC water and applied microbial remediation certifications. The team grows to five technicians and a full equipment cache.

2020

500 properties restored.

Through a tough year for DFW (and everyone else), we cross the 500-property mark. Direct insurance billing now in place with every major carrier.

2023

Three regional hubs.

We stop chasing addresses from one warehouse and stand up three regional caches — north, west, and east — to hit our 30-minute response promise across the metro.

2026

1,400+ homes & businesses.

Still family-owned. Still answering the phone ourselves. New crew on board, but the same shirts, the same standards, the same promise.

What we stand on

Four things we won't compromise on.

01

Show up on time.

If we say 30 minutes, we mean 30 minutes. If we'll be late, we call before the clock runs out — not after.

02

Tell the whole truth.

If a repair would cost less than mitigation, we'll say so. Even when it's less work for us. Especially then.

03

Treat the house like ours.

Floor protection on every job. Doors closed at the end of every day. Trash hauled out, not piled up in the yard.

04

Finish what we start.

One crew from mitigation through rebuild. No handoffs to subcontractors you've never met. One number to call when you have a question.

05

Answer the phone.

A real human, every time. No queue, no recording, no "your call is important to us." It is — so we pick up.

06

Keep it family.

Every person on our crew is treated, trained, and paid like family. Because that's how they treat your home.
