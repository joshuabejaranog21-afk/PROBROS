Common questions

Things people ask first.

If you don't see your question here, call us. We won't put you through a phone tree.

Do you work directly with my insurance?
Yes — we bill insurance directly for most carriers and provide the documentation adjusters need (moisture logs, drying records, line-item estimates). If you're not sure where to start, we'll help you file the claim.
How fast can someone be on site?
Our DFW average is 30 minutes for emergencies. We keep crews and equipment cached across the metro so we can dispatch from whichever location is closest.
What does a free assessment include?
A walk-through with one of our technicians, full photo documentation, moisture readings where relevant, a written scope of work, and an itemized estimate. No obligation, no pressure.
Are you licensed and certified?
Yes. We're IICRC certified across water, fire, and applied microbial remediation. Fully insured, Texas-licensed mold remediation contractor.
Do you handle the rebuild, or just the cleanup?
Both. We start at mitigation and stay through final paint and trim — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, the works. One crew, one contract, one walk-through at the end.
