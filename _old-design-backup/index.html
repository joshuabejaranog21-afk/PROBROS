The story

Two brothers. One promise.

Pro Bros Restoration was founded by Erick and Isai Bejarano — two brothers who watched their own neighbors lose everything to a single burst pipe and decided to do something about it.

For nearly a decade, we've served the Dallas–Fort Worth community with one goal: restore not just the house, but the feeling of being home. Every job, big or small, gets the same care we'd give our own mother's living room.

"We don't just clean up after fires and floods. We sit at kitchen tables with people on the worst day of their year and tell them it's going to be okay — then we make sure it is."

Our process

Six steps. No surprises.

We've refined the same process across hundreds of jobs. You'll know what's happening on day one, day three, and the day we hand back the keys.

01

Inspection & assessment

On-site within 30 minutes. We document everything, scope the loss, and walk you through next steps in plain English.

02

Immediate action

Water extraction, board-up, tarping, containment. Stop the bleed before it gets worse — within hours, not days.

03

Damage removal

Affected drywall, flooring, insulation, and contents removed safely. Hazardous material disposed to code.

04

Cleaning & treatment

HEPA filtration, antimicrobial fogging, soot and odor treatment. Air quality verified before we move on.

05

Repair & restoration

Drywall, paint, trim, flooring, cabinetry. We rebuild to match — and where it makes sense, better than before.

06

Final walkthrough

Punch-list, sign-off, and a follow-up call two weeks later. We're not finished until you tell us we are.
